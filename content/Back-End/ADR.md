**Definition:** **ADR = Architecture Decision Record**, a short document recording an important architectural decision and the reasoning behind it.  
**It helps future developers understand not only what was chosen, but why it was chosen.**

**Examples:**

1. **Decision:** Use PostgreSQL instead of MongoDB. **Reason:** Strong relational transactions are required.
2. **Decision:** Use Kafka for asynchronous communication.
3. **Decision:** Use microservices instead of a monolith for a particular large-scale system.
