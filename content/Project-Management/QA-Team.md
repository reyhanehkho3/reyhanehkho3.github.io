## QA Team — Quality Assurance

**Definition:** The QA team checks whether software **works correctly and meets its requirements** before it reaches users.  
They find bugs through testing and help prevent defects from reaching production.

**Examples:**

1. QA tests whether users can successfully purchase a product.
2. QA tests the website on Chrome, Firefox, and mobile devices.
3. QA verifies that fixing one bug did not break another feature.
