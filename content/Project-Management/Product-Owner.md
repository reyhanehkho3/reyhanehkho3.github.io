**Definition:** A Product Owner is the person responsible for **maximizing the value of a product** and deciding what the development team should prioritize.  
They manage and prioritize the Product Backlog based on customer and business needs.

**Examples:**

1. Decides that “online payment” is more important than “dark mode.”
2. Talks to customers to understand what feature they actually need.
3. Changes the priority of tasks in the development team's backlog.
