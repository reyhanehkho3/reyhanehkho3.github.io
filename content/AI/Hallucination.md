**Definition:** A situation where the model generates incorrect, fabricated, or unsupported information with confidence.

**Simply put:** AI **doesn't know something but answers as if it does**.

**Examples:**

1. Inventing an API that doesn't actually exist.
2. Attributing a fake capability to Python.
3. Inventing a nonexistent article or reference.
