**Definition:** A model designed or optimized for solving complex, multi-step problems that require reasoning.

**Simply put:** These models are generally better suited for tasks requiring **multi-step analysis and reasoning**.

**Examples:**

1. Solving a complex mathematical problem.
2. Analyzing software architecture.
3. Finding the cause of a complicated bug.
