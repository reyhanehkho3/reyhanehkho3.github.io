**Definition:** Information that the Agent maintains during the current Session, usually within the Context of the ongoing execution.

**Simply put:** It is what the Agent **currently has in its working mind**.

**Examples:**

1. Questions you asked a few minutes ago.
2. The result of the most recent Tool Call.
3. Files the Agent examined during the current task.
