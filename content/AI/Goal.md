**Definition:** The result that the Agent should work and make decisions toward achieving.

**Simply put:** Goal says **what ultimately needs to be accomplished**.

**Examples:**

1. “Fix all failing tests.”
2. “Build a REST API.”
3. “Find the cheapest suitable model for the task.”
