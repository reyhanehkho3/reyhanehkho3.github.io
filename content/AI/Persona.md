**Definition:** A collection of behavioral characteristics, communication style, and personality traits defined for an Agent.

**Simply put:** Persona determines **what kind of personality the Agent has and how it behaves**.

**Examples:**

1. “A patient teacher who explains things simply.”
2. “A precise and strict senior programmer.”
3. “A formal and professional assistant.”
