### The simple idea

With Google, you ask:

> “What are the best backend frameworks in 2026?”

and you get a list of websites.

With ChatGPT, you might get:

> “NestJS, Spring Boot, Django, etc…”

With **Perplexity**, you ask the question and it **searches the web, reads relevant sources, and gives you a synthesized answer with citations**. ([Perplexity AI](https://www.perplexity.ai/hub?utm_source=chatgpt.com "Perplexity | AI for the Curious"))

So its main strength is:

**Search → read sources → summarize → show sources**

### Is it another AI model?

Not exactly.

Perplexity is more like an **AI search/research platform**. It can use different underlying AI models and combines them with web search. ([Perplexity AI](https://www.perplexity.ai/hub?utm_source=chatgpt.com "Perplexity | AI for the Curious"))

For example, depending on the feature/plan, it can work with models from companies such as OpenAI and Anthropic, alongside Perplexity's own models.

### Perplexity vs ChatGPT

A rough comparison:

||ChatGPT|Perplexity|
|---|---|---|
|Conversation|⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐|⭐⭐⭐⭐|
|Coding|⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐|⭐⭐⭐⭐|
|Writing|⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐|⭐⭐⭐|
|Web research|⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐|⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐|
|Citations by default|Sometimes|**Very central to the product**|
|Current information|Yes, with web search|**Yes, web-first**|
|Deep research|Yes|**Yes**|
|General-purpose assistant|**Excellent**|Good|
|Search-engine replacement|Good|**Excellent**|

Perplexity itself describes its product as an **“AI answer engine”** that researches the open web and returns cited answers. ([Perplexity AI](https://www.perplexity.ai/hub?utm_source=chatgpt.com "Perplexity | AI for the Curious"))

### When would _you_ use it?

For example, if you're doing a **CS research assignment** and ask:

> “What are the applications of statistics in AI, specifically in model evaluation?”

Perplexity is particularly useful because it can find papers/articles and give you an answer **with the sources attached**, which you can then inspect.

For something like:

> “Explain JWT to me like I'm a beginner.”

I'd generally use **ChatGPT**, because the conversational teaching aspect is more important than web research.

So I'd summarize it as:

> **Google finds pages.  
> ChatGPT helps you think/work.  
> Perplexity researches the web and gives you a cited answer.**

And there's quite a bit of overlap now—ChatGPT itself also has web search/research capabilities.
