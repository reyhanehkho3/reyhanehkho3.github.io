**Definition:** A collection of Agents that collaborate with defined roles to accomplish a larger task.

**Simply put:** Instead of one Agent, you have several Agents working together like a **team**.

**Examples:**

1. A Researcher Agent + a Writer Agent.
2. A Developer + Tester + Reviewer.
3. A Planner + several Specialists.
