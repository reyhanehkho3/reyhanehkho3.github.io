**AI amplifies execution power; if the direction is wrong, errors also become faster and wider.**

### Essential skills:
- Asking questions and clarifying ambiguity;
- Critical thinking and spotting unsupported claims;
- Clear communication with technical and non-technical people;
- Negotiating scope and trade-offs;
- Receiving feedback and review without personal defensiveness;
- Owning the outcome, not delegating responsibility to AI;
- Domain knowledge, product thinking, and understanding risk.

A strong engineer is **T-shaped:** depth in one area and sufficient breadth in testing, data, architecture, product, operations, and AI communication. AI does not replace judgment; judgment determines which problem, under what constraints, and with what level of confidence it should be solved.
