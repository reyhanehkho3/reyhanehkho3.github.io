## **What is a Landing Page?**

A **Landing Page** is a web page that a user arrives at after clicking a link, advertisement, email, or search result.

Its main purpose is usually to encourage the visitor to take a specific action, such as:

- Signing up for an account.
    
- Purchasing a product.
    
- Downloading an application.
    
- Registering for an event.
    
- Requesting a demo.
    

### **Example**

Imagine you see an advertisement for a project management application.

1. You click the advertisement.
    
2. You arrive at a page introducing the application.
    
3. The page explains its features and benefits.
    
4. You see a **Sign Up** button.
    

That page is the landing page.

### **Landing Page vs. Homepage**

|Landing Page|Homepage|
|---|---|
|Focuses on a specific goal|Introduces the overall website|
|Often designed for marketing campaigns|Provides general website navigation|
|Usually has a specific Call to Action (CTA)|May have multiple purposes and links|

### **What is CTA?**

**CTA (Call to Action)** is an element that encourages users to take an action.

Examples:

- Sign Up
    
- Buy Now
    
- Get Started
    
- Download
    
- Contact Us
    

**In simple terms:** A landing page is a page designed to turn visitors into users, customers, or leads by encouraging them to take a specific action.
