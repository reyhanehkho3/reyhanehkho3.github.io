HA is the overall architecture that minimizes downtime.

It can include:

- Replication.
    
- Automatic failover.
    
- Health checks.
    
- Load balancing.
    
- Backup and recovery.
    

Replication alone does not guarantee HA. You also need a way to detect failures and promote a standby.
