Encryption protects database information.

There are two important types:

|Type|Meaning|
|---|---|
|Encryption at rest|Protects database files stored on disk|
|Encryption in transit|Protects communication between applications and the database|

For example, PostgreSQL can use TLS to encrypt client connections.
