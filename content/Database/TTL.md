**Definition:** **TTL = Time To Live**, meaning data is automatically considered expired or removed after a specified amount of time.  
**It is useful when information should only exist temporarily.**

**Examples:**

1. Login session expires after 30 minutes.
2. Password-reset token expires after 15 minutes.
3. Cache entry expires after 10 minutes.
