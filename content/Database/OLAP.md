**Definition:** **OLAP = Online Analytical Processing**, designed for analyzing large amounts of data through aggregations, filtering, grouping, and reporting.  
**Unlike OLTP, OLAP focuses on questions such as "What happened?" rather than frequent individual transactions.**

**Examples:**

1. "What were our total sales for each month?"
2. "Which product category generated the most revenue?"
3. "How many users from each country purchased products this year?"
